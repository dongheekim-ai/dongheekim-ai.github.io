Hi! I am a PhD student in Artificial Intelligence at Korea University, advised by Donghyun Kim. Before starting my PhD, I spent three years as an AI Researcher at VUNO Inc., building deep-learning models for medical imaging. My research spans computer vision, vision-language models, multimodal agents, and perception for physical AI.

Research

My research focuses on multimodal intelligence and visual understanding — teaching models to perceive, reason, and act across vision and language. My recent works explore:

  • Multimodal agents and visual reasoning (ICML'26)
  • Perception for physical AI and embodied task planning (IROS'26)
  • Referring and universal image segmentation (ICCV-W'25, Preprint'24)
  • Self-supervised and domain-adaptive learning (ACCV'24, VISAPP'22)
