Research

MLLMs cannot attend to every region or frame they are shown, so they must locate the few pieces of evidence a question depends on. I study how they find, use, and verify that evidence across space and time:

  • Spatial: 3D spatial reasoning from images, where tools help most as scaffolding for exploration during RL training (ICML'26), and grounding robot manipulation plans in where to act for physical AI (IROS'26).
  • Temporal: structuring hours-long videos into units that retrieval can actually use (NeurIPS'26 E&D).
  • Spatiotemporal (current focus): reasoning about how scenes, objects, and the camera itself change over the course of a video, and whether a model's answer truly rests on what it saw.

Earlier work: medical imaging, image segmentation (ICCV-W'25, Preprint'24), and self-supervised / domain-adaptive learning (ACCV'24).
